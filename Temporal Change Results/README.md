# Temporal Change Detection and Sand Area Analysis

This analysis compares refined sand masks for 25 study locations
around the Abqaiq–Salasil Highway across eight years, 2019–2026.

## Notebook and Inputs

The implementation is in `Temporal_Change_Detection.ipynb`
at the repository root.

Inputs:
- `Texture Morphology Results/masks/<year>/*_refined.tif`
- `RawDataset/metadata.csv`
- Processed multispectral GeoTIFFs for RGB comparison figures
- `Texture Morphology Results/summary.csv` for area consistency checks

Input mask values:
- 0: classified non-sand
- 1: classified sand
- 255: invalid, excluded from analysis

The refined masks are generated by the spectral–texture clustering
and morphological processing approach in `texture_morphology.py`.

## Method

Annual sand area and coverage are calculated for all 200 observations.
Each 10 m × 10 m pixel represents 100 square metres, or 0.0001 km².

Temporal comparisons include:
- 175 consecutive-year comparisons: seven intervals at 25 sites
- 25 full-period comparisons: 2019 versus 2026

Only pixels valid at both dates are compared.

Change-map values:
- 0: persistent non-sand
- 1: persistent sand
- 2: sand gain
- 3: sand loss
- 255: invalid or excluded

Calculations:
- Sand area = sand-pixel count × 0.0001 km²
- Annual sand coverage (%) = sand pixels / valid pixels × 100
- Net change = gain area − loss area
- Total changed area = gain area + loss area
- Changed area (%) = total changed area / common valid area × 100
- Relative sand-area change (%) = (later sand area − earlier sand area)
  / earlier sand area × 100

For temporal comparisons, earlier and later sand areas are measured
over the same common valid pixels. Relative sand-area change is
undefined when the earlier sand area is zero.

## Results Archive

Download and extract `Temporal_Change_Results.zip`.

It contains:
- `tables/annual_sand_area.csv`: 200 annual observation records
- `tables/temporal_change_statistics.csv`: 200 comparison records
- `tables/change_2019_2026.csv`: 25 full-period comparison records
- `change_maps/`: 200 georeferenced classified change maps
- `figures/`: five figures showing annual trends, net change,
  and RGB comparisons
- `analysis_provenance.json`: input version and interpretation notes

RGB examples cover:
- L1: a road-centred site
- L7: the largest classified net decrease
- L17: the largest classified net increase

## Checks and Limitations

The notebook checks:
- Observation counts and available years
- Duplicate site/year records
- Mask values, dimensions, coordinate reference systems, and grids
- Agreement with the existing rounded refinement-area summary
- Consistency between gain, loss, net change, and common valid area

These checks establish computational consistency, not segmentation
accuracy.

Outputs represent changes in classified sand coverage. Segmentation
errors, illumination and reflectance differences, and boundary
processing can contribute to apparent changes.

The imagery has a 10 m output grid; the SWIR bands have native
20 m resolution. Annual observations cannot resolve changes
between acquisition dates.

Results do not independently establish dune migration rates,
road-lane obstruction, or the causes of observed changes.
Interpretation remains subject to segmentation evaluation.
